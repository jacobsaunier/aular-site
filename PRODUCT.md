# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Existing static site: plain HTML and CSS (`index.html`, `impressum.html`, `datenschutz.html`, `css/style.css`), self-hosted Instrument Sans, hosted on GitHub Pages at aular-app.de. No build step, no framework.

## Users

Students at the Medical School Hamburg (MSH) who want to do sport with other students. They arrive on their phone or laptop to learn what Aular is while the app is not yet in the stores. The site is public, but MSH students are the audience it is written for.

## Product Purpose

Aular is a mobile app (iOS and Android) that brings students together through shared sport: find or create sport events, join or invite others, meet in person, and share a Campus Moment afterwards. The website explains the app, states honestly that it is coming soon, and carries the legal and privacy pages (Impressum, Datenschutz) and support contacts. Success: an MSH student understands Aular in one read and trusts it enough to download it at launch.

## Positioning

Verified students only. Sign-up uses a university email address, so visitors meet real people from their own campus instead of strangers in group chats. A generic sport or event app cannot truthfully claim this.

## Operating Context

The app is not yet released. Store entries are "Bald im App Store" / "Bald bei Google Play" and are not links. Currently available only to MSH students. The operator is an individual (Jacob Weber), running Aular as a non-commercial project; this is reflected in the Impressum. Data is hosted in the EU (Ireland).

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed app functions the site describes: sport events (image, date, time, place, participant count, optional participation approval); invite students by username; connect in person via QR code; Campus Moments (post-event photo shared with the hochschule); event chat; report and block profiles; hosts can remove participants; minimum age 18. Contacts: hallo@aular-app.de, support@aular-app.de. Language: German, informal "du" in marketing copy; legal pages stay formally correct. No third-party requests (self-hosted fonts, no analytics or trackers), matching the privacy policy.

## Brand Commitments

Name "Aular" (wordmark "aular." with a period). Existing brand colors: Navy #1E293B, Light #EDEDF5, Ocean Blue #0369A1. Typeface: Instrument Sans. Colors and typeface are binding: future work must keep them.

## Evidence on Hand

Real feature descriptions and legal text only. No users, testimonials, ratings, press, screenshots or store links exist yet. Future work must not invent any of these.

## Product Principles

1. Be honest about status: the app is coming soon, and nothing on the site implies otherwise.
2. Campus trust first: verified students, safety tools and EU data hosting are part of the pitch, not footnotes.
3. Speak plainly to students in German, with no feature-chaos and no hype.
4. Never add tracking or external dependencies that contradict the privacy policy.
5. Legal pages stay correct and easy to reach from every page.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific standard was stated. The current site has a skip link, labeled landmarks and focus styles; keep these as the baseline.
